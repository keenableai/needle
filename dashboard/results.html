Methodology

  • Every engine gets the same query text in each run, one request at a time, so latency is comparable across engines.
  • Queries are made fresh from public sources: News every hour, the other verticals every day. There is no fixed set to overfit.
  • An LLM judge that does not see the engine name scores News and AgenticRare. Scholar and Legal are known-item tasks: a hit is the known paper, court opinion or regulation. Finance checks whether the results contain the known answer from public registries and SEC filings, with an LLM check of results that the text match does not accept.
  • We score what the engine returned: ranking, titles and snippets, with the same 2,000-character snippet budget for every engine. We do not fetch pages and we do not re-rank.
  • A failed search scores zero when the engine caused it (timeout, server error, content filter, a rejected query). We leave it out of the mean only when our own account or plan caused it (auth, quota, rate limit, billing). Reports list both kinds of error.
  • Keenable operates NEEDLE, and the judge has not yet had a systematic human-agreement audit. The queries, prompts and raw results are public, so anyone can score any result again.

Columns

overall
Mean share of the ceiling across the verticals where the engine has a score, last 7 days, with a 95% confidence interval. A dash marks a vertical with no score.
{{VERTICALS}}
Share of the ceiling in each vertical, last 7 days.
$ / 1k queries
Lowest public self-serve price per 1,000 queries, without enterprise quotes; collected {{PRICES_ASOF}}. The plan behind each price is in the price table.
p50 latency, p95 latency
Search latency of successful calls, last 7 days.
unique URLs
Share of the engine's URLs that no other engine family returned for the same query and that are relevant, last 7 days. Engines of one family, such as Exa and Exa Instant, do not count against each other.
