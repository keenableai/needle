Slices

freshstream

Fresh, time-sensitive queries mined hourly from RSS feeds and Google Trends, scored on ranking quality (RBP@5) by an LLM relevance judge.

freshstream — RBP@5

20 fresh queries per run, mean RBP over all queries in each 6-hour window; the hairline marks the metric ceiling

rarestream

Rare-entity queries mined from real search logs — obscure names, technical terms, and long compounds selected by tokenizer difficulty. Scored on ranking quality (RBP@5) by the same LLM relevance judge as freshstream.

rarestream — RBP@5

100 rare-entity queries per daily run; the hairline marks the metric ceiling

companyfill

Company & financial fact-lookups: registry-grounded facts (CEO, HQ, founding year…), quarterly SEC-XBRL facts (filings), and known-item SEC-filing retrieval by accession identity (filingdoc). Companies mixed across cap tiers; ~half the filings/filingdoc queries carry operator syntax ("quoted", site:, date filters). Uniform deterministic answer-recall@5 with an LLM-judge backstop.

gold queries per run across all suites

companyfill — filings recall@5

quarterly facts (net income, operating income, diluted EPS) in top-5 snippets

companyfill — filingdoc recall@5

scholar

Known-item paper retrieval: find a specific paper by its title vs. by a full-text-only detail. Deterministic match on the paper's arXiv / DOI / PMID.

scholar — title-query recall@10

papers found by their (degraded) title

scholar — body-query recall@10

papers found by a full-text-only detail

legal

Known-item legal retrieval: find a specific court opinion by its caption, or a specific CFR section from its substance. Roughly half the queries carry operator syntax ("quoted", site:, date filters). Deterministic match on citation / docket / URL identity.

legal — caselaw recall@5

federal court opinions found by caption-style queries

legal — code recall@5

CFR sections found from their regulatory substance
